What the payments actually add up to

Rent-to-own is priced weekly or bi-weekly, and the total cost to own is far above the sticker price. Work the total before you sign.

Vehicle priceTypical weekly paymentTermTotal paidCost above sticker

$16,000$120 – $160 / wk24 – 36 mo$12,480 – $24,960−$3,520 to +$8,960

Range assumes 104 weeks (2 yr) at the low payment through 156 weeks (3 yr) at the high payment. A 24-month term at the low end can beat the sticker price; the same term at the high end can nearly double it — which end you land on is the whole negotiation.

Colorado protections: rental-purchase agreements fall under C.R.S. 5-10-401 through 5-10-504. The contract must disclose the total cost to own and the early buy-out price, you cannot be charged more than the disclosed total, late fees are capped (a few dollars per late payment, no more than six times in any six-month period), and choosing weekly over monthly payments can legally cost you more. Ask for the total-cost-to-own figure in writing before signing anything.

True lease-to-own & rent-to-own programs

These are the programs that work closer to actual rent-to-own: rental payments that convert to ownership, run through partner dealers.

DriveItAway

National · through participating dealers
Low or no down weekly rental that converts
  • Start by renting weekly, then convert to ownership
  • Reports on-time payments to credit bureaus
  • Must be run through a partner dealership — confirm a Pueblo-area partner before applying
Best fit if you want rent-to-own that builds credit history

Flex — pre-owned lease (Colorado)

Denver · Colorado Springs · Aurora · Fort Collins
$99 start fee 24-month lease, payments not published
  • Leases ~2-year-old vehicles still under factory warranty
  • 20+ brands, approved online through banking partners
  • Not ownership lease — you return the car at the end
Note: unrelated to “Flexcar” below — different company, different product
AC

Acima lease-to-own (retail)

Big O Tires · Good Guys · Pueblo retailers
Weekly / bi-weekly not a vehicle loan
  • Lease-to-own for tires and parts at Pueblo shops — not full vehicles
  • Needs active checking account, $750+/mo income, 3 months history
  • Costs more than the cash price; early purchase option available
Useful only for getting tires/repairs onto a payment plan

Car subscriptions — nearest availability

All-inclusive monthly car use: insurance, maintenance and roadside bundled. None of these operate in Pueblo itself.

SIXT+

80+ US locations · Denver branch
$999 – $1,699 / mo 30-day terms
  • Book a vehicle class, renew every 30 days, cancel with notice
  • Premium brands; pickup at a SIXT branch (delivery in some Denver areas)
  • Closest real subscription to Pueblo
Best bet for month-to-month flexibility in Colorado today

Hertz long-term rental

Denver · Colorado Springs
≈ $1,300 – $4,100 / mo derived from daily rates
  • Denver average $137/day; good-deal rates around $71/day; Lakewood as low as $44/day
  • Monthly rate negotiated directly — the daily × 30 math is an upper bound
  • Insurance and maintenance included in the rental
Cheapest path to “car for a month” without owning anything
